07 · Notes

Flags

  • CRM revenue up 59%. ₹376,571, now 17.0% of store sales (September 11.4%). WhatsApp more than doubled.
  • Email bounces are above 2% again. 7 of 10 sends, 2.04% overall (30 Sep: 0.33%). The rise starts with the 8 Oct send. Clean bounced addresses and send to engaged openers only until it is back under 2%.
  • The two biggest emails earned least. Bestsellers (12,690 sent) earned ₹0. The Gifting resend earned ₹1,349. Engaged-only sends did better.
  • Repeat buyers give the best WhatsApp return. Shipping cut-off to 2+ order customers ran at 46.6×, Diwali Tohfa to 3+ order customers at 33.5×. Kesar to all prospects ran at 0.6×, Sitara v2 at 1.4×.
  • Returning rate fell because new buyers grew. 772 new against 126 returning. Diwali offers and the pop-up pulled first-time buyers. November adds sends written for past buyers.
  • *Data. Days to second order uses the live store database, customers observed for at least a year, same method as September. WhatsApp per-send costs are KwikEngage estimates (₹51,478); spend and ROI use the billed ₹40,842 plus the ₹14,000 platform fee. Alia attributed sales are not added to CRM revenue. Email opens include machine opens.
