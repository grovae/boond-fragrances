07 · Notes

Flags

  • List cleaning worked. Full-list email bounce fell from 3.84% on 5 Sep to 0.33% on 30 Sep after the September suppressions.
  • Engaged sends earn more. ₹2.81 per email to the engaged list vs ₹1.06 to the full list. "What they wrote about our town" earned ₹8.22 per email.
  • Festive 1x batch 2 lost money. ₹649 on ₹1,694 spend. The same cohort had been messaged twice in 48 hours.
  • Email welcome flow is still weak. 2.9% open rate; it still triggers on imported contacts. Move it to new sign-ups only.
  • The pop-up collects phones without emails. 89% of new contacts came via the pop-up, but only 40% left an email. Making email mandatory is the single biggest list lever.
  • *Days to second order is measured on the live store database, which matches customers by customer ID; the audit’s 141 matched by email. Like for like, the median was 103 on the audit date and is 99 now, so second orders are arriving faster. Lifetime repeat rate uses the audit’s own method and is directly comparable.
  • Data. KwikEngage automations export carries no date range; treated as September (cost matches the September billing screen within ₹39). Alia's "attributed sales" ($7,648) is view-through and in dollars; not added to CRM revenue. Email opens include machine opens.
