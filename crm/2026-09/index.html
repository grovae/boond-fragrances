Why we're holding the smaller audience

The full list sold more, but 575 emails bounced across two sends. Past 2%, Gmail starts routing a sender to spam, and that costs every future email, not one. Warmup 1 opened at 26.6%. Five days later the same list opened at 8.3%. That drop is the early warning.

What we're changing

05 · Read with care

What we're not reading too much into.

Open rates

Directional

Where Omnisend can tell the device, almost every open shows as desktop. That isn't how Indian shoppers read email. Privacy tools and inbox scanners are opening emails automatically. We use opens to compare sends, not to count readers.

Low clicks

Expected

0.1–0.6% click rates are normal for story emails with no offer and one soft link. Clicks become the number to watch once product and offer emails start.
